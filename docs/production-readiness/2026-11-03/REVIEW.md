## Five-discipline assessment

**Correctness/code:** The standalone page renders sample claims, and its last-room instructions depend on a host context not present in that document. The Webflow helper is a separate integration, not proof of a working host. **Automation/testing:** no test runner or authored workflow appears in the complete recorded tree; historical Pages success is not a functional gate. **DevOps/operations:** static assets, external module and fonts, hosted origin, deployment ownership, rollback and the CMS are separate failure points; no current availability was measured.

**OWASP/security:** F06 is an origin/source trust-boundary weakness with presentation-state impact only as demonstrated statically. No authentication, authorization, protected-data handling or secret management was established for the external CMS; review them with its owner rather than assuming absence. Review dependency provenance, CSP compatibility, injection surfaces and sensitive data exposure in the actual host. The sampled helper inserts fixed markup and uses `textContent` for guide copy ([below-world.v4.js:161–168](https://github.com/knarayanareddy/proofpress-evidence-portal/blob/6a2adfacca2bba4ee8db6f41b3a9ee67dbb8844c/below-world.v4.js#L161-L168)); this is not a whole-site injection audit.

**UI/UX/accessibility:** Native buttons, station navigation and silent-by-default audio are positive starting points ([index.html:223–240](https://github.com/knarayanareddy/proofpress-evidence-portal/blob/6a2adfacca2bba4ee8db6f41b3a9ee67dbb8844c/index.html#L223-L240)). Test focus order, Space/Enter, screen-reader announcements, motion, reflow and the no-WebGL path; visual markup alone cannot establish conformance.
